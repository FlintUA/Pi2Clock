Settings

Announcements and quiet hours

Off pauses hourly and half-hour announcements until the clock restarts. Requested speech, timer and alarms are not affected.

Quiet hours

Quiet hours mute automatic announcements and postpone birthday melodies for up to 30 minutes. Alarms, timer, music and requested speech still play. Times use Europe/Berlin.

Settings

Bluetooth speakers

How speakers work

Saved speakers connect without searching. Put a new speaker in pairing mode, then search. Searching disconnects the current speaker; afterwards Bluetooth stays off until you press Connect. Disconnect keeps the speaker saved; Forget removes it and its pairing. Up to four speakers can be saved.

Settings

Wi-Fi and Direct

Connection help

Home connects the clock through your router. Direct connects this phone to the network SpeakingClock at 192.168.4.1 without a router. Switching ends the current connection. The Direct password is shown in Phone Control on the clock. After a restart the clock uses home Wi-Fi.

How setup and recovery work

Setup runs in Direct. You have 5 seconds to cancel, then up to 45 seconds to connect; this phone disconnects. On success, join that home Wi-Fi and read the clock address in Phone Control. On failure, SpeakingClock returns with the same password and the previous networks.

Changed credentials turn home trust off, so you may need the pairing code at the new address. Show reveals only the password you are typing; saved passwords are never sent by the clock.

About browser access

Entries are browser permissions, not connected phones. The same browser can have separate Home and Direct entries. Forgetting this browser requires pairing again unless trusted home access applies. Forget all browsers and home trust are changed in Phone Control on the clock.
